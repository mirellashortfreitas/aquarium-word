Welcome to Aquarium World

Welcome to Aquarium World, where an unforgettable underwater adventure awaits visitors of all ages. Explore a variety of fascinating marine habitats and discover the incredible diversity of life beneath the ocean's surface. From colourful coral reefs filled with tropical fish to the mysterious depths of the deep sea, every zone offers something unique to explore.

Meet graceful sea turtles, playful dolphins, curious octopuses, friendly rays, and many other amazing sea creatures. Wander through our themed exhibits, learn about marine ecosystems, and discover how these animals survive in their natural environments.

For younger visitors, the Character Cove features fun, family-friendly sea creatures that make learning about the ocean exciting and interactive. Every area is designed to inspire curiosity while promoting the importance of protecting our oceans.

At selected times throughout the day, visitors can take part in our Animal Feeding Sessions, where you can watch our expert keepers feed a variety of marine animals and learn interesting facts about their behaviour, diet, and daily care.

For an even more memorable experience, our Touch Pool allows guests to gently touch and interact with selected marine species under the close supervision of our trained staff. This hands-on activity gives visitors the opportunity to safely experience the unique textures and movements of some of the ocean's friendliest creatures.

Whether you are visiting with family, friends, or on your own, Aquarium World offers an educational and entertaining experience for everyone. We hope your journey through our underwater world leaves you with unforgettable memories and a greater appreciation for marine life and ocean conservation.

Coral Reef Zone
