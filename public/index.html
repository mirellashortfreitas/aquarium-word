Entry to multiple attractions with Aquarium World

Home to over 1000 species across 10 themed zones, there's so much to explore at Aquarium World:

  • Adventure - Journey through icy Arctic and Antarctic landscapes and get up close with our colony of Gentoo Penguins
  • Ocean Invaders - Step into an immersive jellyfish experience spanning several unique underwater environments
  • Coral Kingdom - Dive into a breathtaking underwater world and discover our thriving living coral reef
  • Rockpool Discovery - Uncover what lies beneath the surface of local seas and meet the creatures found closer to home.
