Frequently Asked Questions

Opening Times: Open every day from 9:00am to 6:00pm.

Is Aquarium World suitable for families?

Yes, our zones and exhibits are designed for visitors of all ages.

Do you offer guided tours?

We run scheduled talks and guided walkthroughs throughout the day.

Is the aquarium accessible?

We provide step-free access, lifts, and clear signage. Staff are available to assist.

Can I bring food and drink?

Food and drink are only allowed in exhibit areas if purchased in our café, or for children under 2 years old

Do you offer tickets online?

Yes, tickets can be booked in advance through our website to skip the queue on arrival.

Is there parking available on site?

Yes, we have a car park located just outside the main entrance, with accessible bays close to the doors.

How long does a visit usually take?

Most visitors spend between 2 and 3 hours exploring all of our zones, though you're welcome to stay all day.

Are pets allowed inside the aquarium?

For everyone safety, pets are not permitted, with the exception of registered assistance animals supporting visitors with disabilities.

Is photography allowed during the visit?

Absolutely — we encourage you to take photos throughout your visit. Flash photography is discouraged near certain tanks to avoid disturbing the marine life.

Do you offer group or school discounts?

Yes, discounted rates are available for school trips and groups of 10 or more. Please contact us in advance to arrange your visit.
